Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

Two lines: line 1 warns my past self about one specific moment — deleting Grandma's voicemail on my eighteenth birthday — in a single sentence; line 2 says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Do not delete the voicemail from Grandma on your eighteenth birthday just to free up space, because her voice saying your name is the one recording you will spend years trying to remember.
> Losing it taught me that storage is cheap and voices are not, so now I keep every message from the people I love — even the ones I think I will never need again.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
